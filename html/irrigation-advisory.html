System Overview

The system analyzes real-time field data to compute crop ET (evapotranspiration) and track essential soil moisture levels of H2O. Precise calculations help schedule target water volume inputs measured in m3 or cm3 depending on field plot size.

Daily Irrigation Recommendations

Key Water Conservation Practices

To maximize field efficiency, adopt sustainable techniques such as drip irrigation networks, organic mulching, and nighttime schedule automation to conserve groundwater reserves.
